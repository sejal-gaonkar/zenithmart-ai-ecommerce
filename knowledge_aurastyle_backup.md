## 2. Order Management & Tracking

### 2.1 Order Confirmation
- Immediately after placing an order, customers receive an automated confirmation email containing an **Order Number** (format: `#AS-XXXXX`, e.g., `#AS-84920`).
- If no confirmation email is received within 15 minutes, customers should verify spam/junk folders or check order history in their AuraStyle account dashboard.

### 2.2 Order Tracking
- Once an order leaves our fulfillment fulfillment centers (located in Columbus, OH and Reno, NV), a shipment notification is emailed with a carrier tracking link (UPS, FedEx, or USPS).
- Tracking information typically updates within 24 hours of package handoff to the carrier.
- Customers can also track shipments anytime at `www.aurastyle-shop.com/track` by entering their order number and email address.

### 2.3 Modifying or Cancelling an Order
- **Cancellation Window:** Orders enter automated fulfillment rapidly. Customers can cancel or change an order within **45 minutes** of submission by clicking "Cancel Order" in their account or contacting live chat immediately.
- **Address Changes:** Shipping addresses can only be modified within that same 45-minute window. Once an order is "Processing" or "Fulfilled", AuraStyle cannot alter shipping addresses. Carriers (UPS My Choice / FedEx Delivery Manager) can sometimes reroute packages directly upon customer request.
- **Adding Items to Existing Orders:** Items cannot be added to an already placed order. Customers must place a separate order.

## 4. Returns, Exchanges & Refunds Policy

### 4.1 Standard 30-Day Return Window
- Customers have **30 calendar days** from the verified carrier delivery date to request a return or exchange.
- **Extended Holiday Policy:** Purchases made between November 1 and December 24 may be returned or exchanged until January 31 of the following year.

### 4.2 Item Condition Requirements
To qualify for a refund or exchange:
- Items must be unwashed, unworn, undamaged, with all original tags attached.
- Shoes and footwear must be returned in their original undamaged shoe box, placed inside an outer shipping box (no shipping labels taped directly to the shoe box).
- Home textiles and bedding must be unwashed in original packaging.

### 4.3 Non-Returnable / Final Sale Items
The following categories are strictly **Final Sale** and cannot be returned or exchanged:
- Intimate apparel, swimwear, and bodysuits (due to hygiene regulations).
- Face masks and personal grooming care products.
- Monogrammed, custom-embroidered, or customized items.
- Items purchased from the "Clearance / Warehouse Outlet" marked as "Final Sale".
- Digital or physical Gift Cards.

### 4.4 Return Fees & Refund Options
Customers initiate returns via our self-service portal at `www.aurastyle-shop.com/returns`.
1. **Option A — Store Credit / Gift Card:** **100% Free return shipping**. A digital store credit bonus of an additional $5.00 is credited once the package is scanned by the carrier. Store credits never expire.
2. **Option B — Refund to Original Payment Method:** A flat return shipping and handling fee of **$5.99** is deducted from the final refund amount.
3. **Exchanges (Size/Color swap):** **100% Free**. Once the carrier scans the returning package, the replacement size/color order is dispatched automatically.

### 4.5 Refund Processing Time
- Once our warehouse receives the returned package, inspection takes **2 to 3 business days**.
- Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days**, depending on the issuing bank.

## 5. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Deliveries

### 5.1 Damaged Upon Arrival
- If an item arrives broken, defective, or incorrect, customers must notify support within **7 calendar days** of delivery.
- Customer should provide:
  1. Order number (`#AS-XXXXX`)
  2. Clear photos of the damage/defect or wrong item label
  3. Photo of the outer shipping box condition
- **Resolution:** AuraStyle sends an immediate, complimentary replacement with expedited shipping at zero cost, or issues an immediate 100% full refund including all original shipping fees. Customers do not pay return shipping for damaged or incorrect goods.

### 5.2 Lost or Stolen Packages
- If tracking shows "Delivered" but the customer cannot locate the package:
  - Check surrounding areas, porches, back gates, building leasing offices, and neighbors.
  - Wait 24 hours, as carriers occasionally mark packages delivered prior to physical drop-off.
  - If still missing after 24 hours, contact `support@aurastyle.com`. AuraStyle will initiate an official carrier tracer claim and dispatch a replacement or issue a full refund.

## 9. Sizing, Materials & Product Care

### 9.1 Sizing Help
- Sizing charts with measurements in both inches and centimeters are provided on every product page.
- Our garments generally fit true-to-size (standard US sizing). If an item runs small or oversized, a "Fit Note" is prominently displayed on the product description page.

### 9.2 Leather Goods & Handbags Warranty
- All AuraStyle genuine leather bags, backpacks, and luggage come with an automatic **1-Year Limited Warranty** covering hardware failures (zippers, buckles, snaps) and stitching defects under normal use.
- Warranty does not cover accidental damage, improper water exposure, pet damage, or natural aging of leather (patina).

### 9.3 Apparel Care Instructions
- For organic cotton and linen garments: Machine wash cold with similar colors on delicate cycle; tumble dry low or line dry to prevent shrinkage.
- Wool and cashmere knits: Hand wash cold with wool-safe detergent, reshape and lay flat to dry. Do not hang knitwear.
