# ZenithMart Support Knowledge (DEMO STORE - fictional products, prices, stock and ratings)
## Shipping
- Standard shipping: 3-5 business days. Fee Rs 49; FREE on orders of Rs 499 or more.
- ZenithFast: 1-2 business days, Rs 99.
## Payment
- Demo store: payment is simulated. No card numbers, CVVs, passwords or OTPs are ever collected.
## Order status
- Orders move Pending -> Shipped -> Delivered. Every order has an order ID (ZMyymmddNNNNN) and a tracking ID (ZMTRK...).
## Cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled only while Pending (before shipping). Refund: 5-7 business days to the original method (demo).
## Returns and exchanges
- Available after delivery for eligible products only (each product page shows return eligibility).
- Not returnable: mobile accessories (chargers, cables), skincare and personal care items.
- Refunds are processed 5-7 business days after the return is received (demo).
## Categories
- Apparel, Footwear, Electronics, Grooming, Accessories.
## Support
- Human help: support@zenithmart.example (demo address).
